Privacy Policy — Meow Grid

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Developer: Tudi Technology

Contact: privacy@modmenux.com

Meow Grid (the "App") is a single-player logic puzzle game for Android.

This policy is short because the App collects nothing.

1. We do not collect any data

The App does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal

information or usage data — not by us, and not by any third-party SDK.

Specifically, the App has:

  • **no advertising** of any kind
  • **no analytics or crash reporting SDK**
  • **no account, login, e-mail or phone number**
  • **no in-app purchases and no payment processing**
  • **no social, chat or user-generated content features**

There is no server that stores your data, because there is no data to store.

2. Everything stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, collected pictures and settings are written to the

App's private storage area on your own device only. Nothing is uploaded

anywhere.

This local data is deleted when you uninstall the App. You can also erase it

at any time from inside the App: Settings → Reset all progress.

3. Permissions

The App requests only the permission it technically needs to run:

PermissionWhy
`android.permission.INTERNET`Included by the Flutter runtime. The App makes **no network requests** of its own.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, calendar,

photos, camera, microphone, files, SMS, call logs, or your device's

advertising identifier.

4. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for all ages. Because the

App collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. There

is nothing to opt out of and nothing to delete on our side.

5. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Data-protection laws give you rights of access, correction, deletion,

portability and objection over your personal data.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to

disclose, correct, export or erase. The only data that exists is the local

save file on your own device, which you fully control and can delete from

inside the App at any time.

6. Data retention

Not applicable — no data is retained by us.

7. International transfers

Not applicable — no data leaves your device.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App ever adds a feature that handles data, this

policy will be updated first, and the change will be disclosed in the App's

Google Play "What's new" section before that version is released.
