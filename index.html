Privacy Policy — Meow Grid

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Developer: Tudi Technology

Contact: privacy@modmenux.com

This policy explains how the mobile game Meow Grid (the "App") handles

information. It applies to the Android version of the App distributed on

Google Play.

1. Information we collect ourselves

**We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal

information.**

  • No account, sign-in, e-mail address or phone number is required.
  • Your puzzle progress, collected stickers and settings are stored **only on

your own device** and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

  • The App does not ask for your location, contacts, calendar, photos, camera,

microphone, SMS or call logs.

2. Advertising (third-party SDK)

The App is free and is supported by advertising delivered by Google AdMob.

To select and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and

process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement
Device information (device model, OS version, language, screen size)ad compatibility and rendering
IP address (used to derive approximate, city-level location)ad relevance, fraud prevention
Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)ad performance measurement

We (the developer) do not receive this data in identifiable form; we only

receive aggregated, anonymous revenue and performance reports from Google.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
  • <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

3. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is **not directed to children

under the age of 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from

children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us

and we will act to remove it.

4. Data security

All data transmitted by the advertising SDK is sent over encrypted connections

(HTTPS/TLS). Because we do not operate a user database, there is no server-side

store of your personal data to breach.

5. Your choices and rights

  • **Reset or delete your advertising ID:** Android *Settings → Privacy →

Ads* (path varies by device) lets you delete or reset your advertising ID,

and opt out of personalised advertising.

  • **Delete all App data:** uninstall the App. All locally stored progress is

removed with it.

  • **Consent (EEA/UK):** where required by law, a consent message is shown

before personalised ads are served, and you may decline.

6. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, ad-related processing is based on your consent

(Art. 6(1)(a)) collected through the consent flow, and on **legitimate

interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) in fraud prevention and service security.

7. Data retention

We retain no personal data. Advertising data is retained by Google according

to Google's own retention policies.

8. International transfers

Ad-related data may be processed on servers located outside your country,

including in the United States, under Google's standard contractual

safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, where

the change is material, note it in the App's Google Play "What's new"

section.
